The Experience

Where sport, connection,
and atmosphere come together effortlessly.

This summer, padel arrives at Ox Ridge. Not as just another sport, but as a new social ritual for the Darien community.

Designed as a highly curated, limited-time three-court and lounge pop-up — where competition is real, but never intimidating. Where you come for a match, and stay for the people.

Inspired by the best clubs, we've built this experience around a single idea: belonging.
