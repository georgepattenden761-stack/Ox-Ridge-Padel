Book & Ratings — Powered by Serve

At Ox Ridge Padel, community is everything — and what makes a community great is trust. Before you even step on court, you should know that the clinic or social you're joining will be with players at your level, and that it's going to be a good, fair workout. That's why we built the club around Serve.

Serve is our platform for both court booking and player ratings, so your scheduling and skill-matching live in one place. After each match, players log their scores and Serve's algorithm updates their rating automatically — no self-reporting, no guesswork, just ratings that reflect how you actually play.

Why does that matter? Because accurate ratings mean every clinic, social and open play session can be grouped by real skill level. When you book in, you can trust you'll be matched with players around your ability — not too easy, not overwhelming, just competitive enough to be fun and push you forward.

That trust in the level-matching is what lets you relax into the social side of the club. You show up knowing the match will be good for you specifically — and that's when the real community happens.

Our Pros

Meet the coaches behind every clinic, social and session at Ox Ridge Padel.

Jake

Bio

Born in Yorkshire and raised in Portugal, Jake grew up playing tennis before making the switch to padel over 10 years ago. After university, he shifted his focus from competing to coaching and has spent the last three years coaching full-time. To deepen his understanding of the sport, he's spent time learning from top Spanish and Argentinian coaches, helping him make a smooth transition into coaching. He's also worked with some of Great Britain's top junior and senior players, helping players of all levels develop their game and confidence on court.

Coaching Style

High-energy, fast-paced sessions focused on building confidence, refining technique, and making every lesson enjoyable.

Specialties

Defence, consistency, and tactical awareness.

Typical Sessions

Dynamic sessions combining technical drills, match scenarios, and point play. Jake's coaching philosophy is simple: build a strong defensive game and consistent foundations first, and the attacking game will naturally follow.

Paulo

Bio

Paulo is a 29-year-old Portuguese padel coach and player. He's been hooked on racket sports since he was a kid and hasn't managed to stop since. Over the last 10 years, he's played and coached in different countries across Europe, helping players of all levels improve their game.

Coaching Style

Padel is meant to be enjoyed. Paulo wants his players to have fun while learning, develop an understanding of how to build points, and play with confidence and aggression when the moment is right. Improvement comes naturally when you're enjoying the game.

Specialties

Tactical awareness & point construction; aggressive attacking play; match strategy and decision making; technical development for all levels; building confidence on court; fun and engaging coaching sessions; game-based learning.

Typical Lessons

Paulo's sessions are high-energy, engaging, and built around real game situations. He combines technical drills, tactical exercises, and match play to help players develop both their skills and understanding of the game. He wants players to feel confident on court, trust their decisions, and play with freedom — knowing when to be patient and when to be aggressive.

Interested in training with our pros? Check the weekly schedule to see when Jake and Paulo are on court.
